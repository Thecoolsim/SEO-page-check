Fill in the fields, or import a page's HTML source. Works for articles and video pages (YouTube or Vimeo). Each check is rated good, medium or bad and updates as you type.

Import from a page's source
  1. Open the page in your browser.
  2. Open the source: Ctrl+U on Windows or Linux, Cmd+Option+U on a Mac (in Safari, turn on the Develop menu first).
  3. Select all, copy, and paste below.
